[SLAYT] Kronik böbrek hastalığı- evreleme (KDIGO-2012): Revised chronic kidney disease classification based upon GFR and albuminuria. GFR stages (mL/min/1.73 m2) | Terms: G1 >90 Normal or high ; G2 60 to 89 Mildly decreased ; G3a 45 to 59 Mildly to moderately decreased ; G3b 30 to 44 Moderately to severely decreased ; G4 15 to 29 Severely decreased ; G5 <15 Kidney failure (add D if treated by dialysis). Albuminuria stages AER (mg/day) | Terms: A1 <30 Normal to mildly increased (may be subdivided for risk prediction) ; A2 30 to 300 Moderately increased ; A3 >300 Severely increased (may be subdivided into nephrotic and non-nephrotic for differential diagnosis, management and risk prediction)
