## Soru
[DAKTİLO] Ketoasidoza yaklaşım: Hiperglisemik aciller tedavi hedefleri:
## Cevap
[DAKTİLO] Sıvı açığını ve doku perfüzyonunu düzeltmek, hiperglisemi ve serum osmalalitesini normale getirmek ve altta yatan sebebi tedavi etmektir.
[DAKTİLO] 4.0-5.0 mEq/l
[DAKTİLO] (akış şeması; üstten iki ok aşağı iner, kutular:) [Orta kutu] K+ 3.5-5.0 mEq/l ise: Her litre sıvıya 10-30 mEq K+ ; Hedef K+ düzeyi: 4.0-5.0 mEq/l. [Sağ kutu] K+ >5.0 mEq/l ise: K+ verme. Ancak, K+ <5.0 mEq/l oluncaya dek 2 saatte bir ölç. [Sol kutu, alt] K+ <3.5 mEq/l ise: insülin verme. 40 mEq/st K+ ; Hedef K+ >3.5 mEq/l (kutu bir sonraki sayfada devam eder). [Alt geniş kutu] Serum potasyum düzeyini ölç. (kutu içinde soluk gri yazı:) Hastanın şuuru düzelip oral almaya başlayınca SC insüline geç. SC insülini iv insülin kesilmeden 2 saat önce yap
[SLAYT] (İngilizce akış şeması, 4 sütun) IV Fluids: Administer 0.9% NaCl at 500-1000 mL/h in the first 2-4 hours → Evaluate corrected serum Na+‡ → [High / Normal → 0.45% NaCl (250-500 ml/hr) depending on hydration state] ; [Low → 0.9% NaCl (250-500 ml/hr) depending on hydration state] → When serum glucose reaches 200 mg/dL, for DKA or HHS, change to 5% dextrose with 0.45% NaCl at 150-250 ml/hr. Bicarbonate: [pH ≥ 6.9 → No HCO3-] ; [pH < 6.9 → 100mmol in 400ml H2O + 20mEq KCL, infuse for 2 hours → Repeat every 2 hours until pH ≥ 7. Monitor serum K+ every 2 hrs.]. Insulin: [IV Route → 0.1 U/Kg IV bolus → 0.1 U/Kg/hr IV infusion] ; [SC Route (*) → 0.2 U/Kg SC bolus → 0.2 U/Kg SC every 2 Hours] → Check serum or point of care glucose every 1-2 hours → [DKA: When serum glucose reaches 200 mg/dl, reduce regular insulin infusion to 0.05 U/kg/hr IV. Keep serum glucose between 150 and 200 mg/dl until resolution of DKA. For SC protocol, reduce insulin to 0.1 U/Kg SC every 2 hours.] ; [HHS: When serum glucose reaches 250 mg/dl, reduce regular insulin infusion to 0.05 U/kg/hr IV. Keep serum glucose ~ 200 mg/dl until patient is mentally alert.] → Transition to SC insulin when patient is alert and able to eat Overlap with IV and basal insulin should be at least 2 hours. Potassium: [K+ <3.3 mEq/L → Hold insulin and give 10-20 mEq /hr Until K+ > 3.3 mEq/L] ; [K+ >5.2 mEq/L → Do not give K+, but check serum K+ every 2 hrs.] ; [K+ = 3.3-5.2 mEq/L → Give 20 - 30 mEq K+ in each liter of IV fluid to keep serum K+ between 4-5 mEq/L]
