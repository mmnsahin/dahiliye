## Cevap
[SLAYT] (slayt, 3 sütun: CONDITION | DIAGNOSIS | BONE ALTERATIONS; solda kemik şeması ve hücre resimleri) B cell → Normal B plasma cells secrete immunoglobulin. MGUS → Diagnosis: • Serum monoclonal protein (≤ 3 g/dL) • Bone marrow plasma cell infiltration (≤10%) • No end-organ damage ; Bone alterations: • Increased risk of fractures • Changes in bone density. Multiple Myeloma → Diagnosis: • Serum monoclonal protein (≥3 g/dL) • Bone marrow plasma cell infiltration (≥ 10%) • End-organ damage ; Bone alterations: • Osteolytic bone lesions • Increased risk of fractures • Spinal cord compression • Bone-related pain
